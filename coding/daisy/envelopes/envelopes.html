Envelopes describe how sounds change over time. These changes can be of amplitude, frequency, or pitch. The most common kind of is ADSR (attack, decay, sustain, and release). With those four parameters being what controls amplitude over time. The first envelope was created by American engineer Robert Moog, trying to recreate the dynamic articulation of natural sounds. In essence, he used a capcitor to control voltage, tying voltage release to whatever he wanted to alter over time.
