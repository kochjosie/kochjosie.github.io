audio engineering and synthesizers

Here's where I'll post my progress as I learn to use Daisy. As of late June 2026, I'm working with the both the Seed and the Pod, so anything I post here will use either. However, I'm going to do my best to document everything I learn as a beginner to anything in the embedded space, hopefully helping other beginners as well. It's a relatively niche device, so tutorials are limited. I'll do my best to make it easier for others. I try my best to provide the most basic recipe I can for each topic.

Note: All Daisy tutorials will be done in C++, not Oopsy or Arduino. I learn best by doing creative projects with the most bare, least abstracted tools I can handle with my current level of knowledge. If this is not ideal for you, I apologize but hope you can learn something from here anyways.
